## About Me
I am Junteng Liu, a first-year PhD candidate at the Department of Computer Science and Engineering, Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (HKUST), advised by Prof. Yangqiu Song. I graduated from Shanghai Jiao Tong University (SJTU) in June 2024 with a Bachelor of Engineering (BEng) in Computer Science, where I was a member of the Zhiyuan Honor Scholarship Program. My research interests include Natural Language Processing and Machine Learning, with a focus on LLM Reasoning and Reinforcement Learning, VLM Hallucination, and LLM Truthfulness and Interpretability.

### Academic Background
- **2024 - Present**: PhD in Computer Science, Hong Kong University of Science and Technology
- **2020 - 2024**: BEng in Computer Science, Shanghai Jiao Tong University (Zhiyuan Honor Scholarship Program)
